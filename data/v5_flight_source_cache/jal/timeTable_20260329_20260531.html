Information

  • The airline code shown before the flight number indicates the following carrier.

    JAL Japan Airlines (Japan Airlines, J-AIR, Japan Air Commuter, Hokkaido Air System)

    JTA Japan Transocean Air

    RAC Ryukyu Air Commuter

  • Codeshare flights with FDA (Fuji Dream Airlines) are operated by FDA aircraft and cabin attendants.
  • Codeshare flights with AMX (Amakusa Airlines) are operated by AMX aircraft and cabin attendants.
  • Codeshare flights with ORC (Oriental Air Bridge) are operated by ORC aircraft and cabin attendants.

  • Changes in departure/arrival times listed in the “Note” column

    Early/late departure
    If the “Departure” time changes, the “Arrival” time will also change accordingly.

    Example: Flight listed as “Departure 12:00 Arrival 13:00” departs 5 minutes early → “Departure 11:55 Arrival 12:55”

    Early/late arrival
    If the “Arrival” time changes, the “Departure” time will remain the same.

    Example: Flight listed as “Departure 12:00 Arrival 13:00” arrives 5 minutes late → “Departure 12:00 Arrival 13:05”
