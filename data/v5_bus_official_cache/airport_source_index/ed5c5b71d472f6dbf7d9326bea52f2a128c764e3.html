Express Bus & Route Bus

Haneda Airport is connected to Tokyo, Kanagawa, Chiba, Saitama, and other locations in the Kanto region by airport bus. Please see the bus route map below for the main routes.

Passengers departing from Haneda Airport are advised to confirm bus schedules via the respective operators’ websites or at Haneda Airport Passenger Terminal. For Terminals 1 and 2, tickets can be purchased at the automatic ticket machines. At Terminal 3, tickets are available from either the machines or the bus ticket counters. Please ensure you purchase your ticket prior to boarding.

When taking the express bus, please do not place power banks in checked luggage; keep them with you inside the vehicle. This is because power banks use lithium-ion batteries, which may catch fire or break due to high temperatures or impacts. We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

See the Haneda Airport Garden website for information on buses departing from Haneda Airport Garden.
