About bus

 

Long-Distance Highway Buses　*Reservations required in advance

 

Bus Stop：⓪
Tickets for these buses are not sold at the airport. To make a reservation, please use that company’s website.

 

* The long-distance bus waiting room is located next to bus stop ⓪.

 

Airport Limousine Buses

 

Bus Stop：①～⑤、⑧～⑬
Purchase tickets from the ticket vending machine, or prepare your transit IC card, then wait at the proper bus stop for your destination.

 

* Some bus companies do not accept IC cards.

 

Short-Distance Local Buses

 

Bus Stop：⑥⑦
Pay the fare while on the bus.

 

* Each piece of information was provided by the individual bus operator, but the content is not guaranteed to be accurate. When possible, please contact the individual bus operator directly.

* The times are measured from Osaka Itami Airport. There may be delays depending on the road conditions.
