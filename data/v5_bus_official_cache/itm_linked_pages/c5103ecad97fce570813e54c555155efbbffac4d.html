Notes on booking

  • Tickets will be sold in advance from 9:00 a.m. on the same day of the month before boarding date (or the last day of the previous month if not the same day of the previous month).
    The reservation start time is the same for all counters, telephones, and Internet.
  • The purchase deadline varies depending on the reservation method. Please note that reservations will be canceled automatically after the deadline.
  • If you lose your ticket, you will not be able to reissue it.
  • Ticket refunds will be made only if requested before the departure time of the flight.
