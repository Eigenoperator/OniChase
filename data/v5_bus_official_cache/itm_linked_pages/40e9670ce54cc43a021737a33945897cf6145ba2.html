Business

With the three airports of Kansai International Airport (“KIX”), Osaka Itami Airport (“ITAMI”), and Kobe Airport (“KOBE”) as our bases, we have been engaging in various business activities, such as support for the inauguration of service for airlines and support for opening stores as tenants.

Main Business of Kansai Airports

  • Kansai International Airport

    Official Website

    As an around-the-clock offshore international airport, Kansai International Airport functions as a hub connecting Japan with the rest of the world. Serving users from around the world, it is an optimal airport for business use and international tourism.

    • Airport Information
    • Business Information

  • Osaka Itami Airport

    Official Website

    Osaka Itami Airport is an urban airport dedicated to domestic flights connecting the Kansai region and the rest of Japan. It is convenient for business travelers and families and is even visited by many people other than air passengers.

    • Airport Information
    • Business Information

  • Kobe Airport

    Official Website

    Kobe Airport is an offshore airport in Kobe City. Taking advantage of its domestic flight network, it will begin to serve international charter flights in 2025. This is expected to allow the airport to provide users with increased business opportunities.

    • Airport Information
    • Business Information
